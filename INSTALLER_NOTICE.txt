PRIVACY
Settings, trails, saved skins and history stay on this computer. Provider
credentials are protected with Windows DPAPI.

The app connects to GitHub for updates and to the server/map services selected
by the user. Npcap reads the game's outbound UDP packets locally; raw packets
are not uploaded. Anonymous analytics and the friend-position relay are off by
default. The relay is used only after the user enables its sharing control.

Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/thienvyma/hubisle/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
